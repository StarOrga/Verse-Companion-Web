## What to change

1. **Veto card (s6): the decision object is the hero.** What matters most is the KI's chosen text
   („Propulsore principale“) together with what it means („Haupttriebwerk“). Make that the one large element of the
   card (largest type on the card; the back-translation directly attached to it, clearly secondary but readable).
   Then the two answers. Everything else shrinks or goes.
2. **Cut the line count hard — target ≤ 5 visual lines on the veto card** (header line, hero, back-translation,
   answers, one quiet footer). Ways: merge the meta into one line („IT · unsicher · 1 von 3“ or the ⚠ on the hero),
   put the reason „Schiffsname entfernt“ as a quiet suffix of the back-translation line or behind the ⚠ hint,
   drop the „LINKS / ODER / RECHTS“ direction row (the answer buttons carry the arrow keys as small kbd), drop the
   consequence sub-lines („Karte kommt in deinen Stapel“, „KI-Text bleibt“, „danach nächste KI-Karte“) — they may
   live in the „Alle Tasten ?“ help or as a tooltip on the answer — and merge the footer into one quiet line.
3. **No overlapping tooltip / cut text** (the „-Link:…“ tooltip covered „…en Text selbst“). Nothing may cover card
   text at rest.
4. **Apply the same rule to every card of the round (s2 convention, s3 term, s4 after-answer):** count the lines,
   make the most important element clearly the largest (for a question card: the two option texts; for s4: the
   chosen text + the daily-goal step), and trim the rest the same way. The user's earlier wish stays: contents are
   fine, the card must be calm and lightweight.
5. Add one annotation on the s6 hero: „Wichtigstes zuerst: KI-Text groß, Bedeutung direkt darunter, alles andere
   leise — so?“
