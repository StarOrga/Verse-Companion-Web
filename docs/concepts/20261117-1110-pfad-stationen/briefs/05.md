## Why this round exists (say it plainly on the page, in German, without excuses and without drama)

The owner clicked "Mit Feedback implementieren" on round 4. Everything of the approved scope was built and then
checked by an independent agent in the real app, signed in, desktop and phone. That check found one thing no round
had shown: **the two stations of the mocks exist in the app only on blueprint pages and on preview pages of
upcoming ships, and there only when the reader arrives from the HQ or from a set.** Item pages and ship pages do
not use that control at all. They carry an older card of their own ("Haben?" / "Dein Lager" / "Dein Exemplar"),
decided in an earlier concept. So on an item or a ship page the owner can today neither see the Operation of a wish
nor assign one, and the one-line place strip of the ownership station ("2× im Inventar · Lorville +1 weiterer Ort")
is rendered nowhere. Rounds 1–4 drew "the detail page" without saying which page that is; that belongs to the
concept, not to the owner. This round closes it: one design choice and three small questions. Nothing else is open.

## The two designs (competing; the owner picks one)

Both designs show the **same three states on the same real page furniture**, so they can be compared screen by
screen. Draw enough of the real page around the card that the place is recognisable (item page: the side panel
`aside` to the right of the item picture, the card is its first element, the tabs follow under it; ship page,
classic view: the card sits directly under the ship picture). Example data as in round 4: Karna Rifle, Operationen
"Bodentrupp ausrüsten" (1 von 4 Einsätzen), "Vorrat Lazarett"; ship example "Cutlass Black", Operation "Flotte
ausbauen" (2 von 5 Einsätzen).

### Design `karte` — nav label "Karte wächst", motto e.g. "Die Karte bleibt, die Leisten kommen dazu" (recommended)

The existing card keeps its frame, its small caps title, its rows and its icon actions (note, pin, remove). It
gains the strips and the button of round 4 as further rows of the same card. Cheapest change, nothing the owner
knows moves. Trade-off to state: the card gets taller, and wish and ownership are rows of one box instead of two
boxes side by side.

### Design `kaesten` — nav label "Zwei Kästen", motto e.g. "Überall dieselben zwei Kästen"

The card goes. Item and ship pages show exactly the two stations approved in round 4 (the control the blueprint
page already has), in the card's place. Note, pin and remove of the old card move into the head of the ownership
station as three icon buttons; "Im HQ zeigen" is the link at the end of the ownership strip ("Im Inventar

other), and the build is larger.

## The analysis view — `r5-wo`, nav label "Analyse", three small questions

Briefing first (the "Stand" above, then "Fest steht"), then exactly these three decisions. Each with a
recommendation preselected, one sentence of consequence per option, no timing questions.

1. `r5-d-bauplan` — "Bauplan- und Vorschau-Seiten: wann zeigen sie die zwei Kästen?" Today: only when you come
   from the HQ or a set; otherwise a small pill without "Zuordnen".
   - `gewuenscht` (recommended) "Sobald etwas gewünscht oder gesammelt ist" — the full form appears when there is
     something to say or to assign; an untouched page stays quiet with the pill.
   - `immer` "Immer, wenn du angemeldet bist" — one look, but every blueprint page gets the big control.
   - `heute` "Wie heute, nur aus dem HQ oder einem Set" — nothing changes; assigning from the Codex stays out of
     reach on most visits.
2. `r5-d-einfach` — "Einfache Gegenstände ohne eigene Bühne (zum Beispiel Quantanium): heute hat die Seite gar
   keine Wunsch-Steuerung. Soll sie eine bekommen?"
   - `ja` (recommended) "Ja, dieselbe Steuerung unter dem Kopf der Seite" — every Codex page of a thing you can
     wish for works the same way.
   - `nein` "Nein, nur in Listen, in der Suche und im HQ" — the page stays as it is.
3. `r5-d-holo` — "Schiffe im Holotisch: dort gibt es keine Karte, der Wunsch steckt im Hangar-Menü. Wo zuordnen?"
   - `menue` (recommended) "Im Hangar-Menü unter „Wunschliste“" — the menu shows the Operation line and
     "Zuordnen"; no need to leave the 3D view.
   - `klassisch` "Nur in der klassischen Ansicht" — the Holotisch stays as it is; the menu only shows the badge.
