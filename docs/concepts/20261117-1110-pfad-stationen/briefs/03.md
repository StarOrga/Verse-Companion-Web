## The rule set the round shows (thought through by the session — show it, and ask only the open parts)

Invariant: for one item, the pieces assigned to Operationen never exceed the wished quantity. A wish of 5 with 3
assigned has **2 free** pieces and **3 assigned** ones. Three things can break the invariant: lowering the wish,
removing the wish, and the wish being fulfilled.

1. **Free pieces go first, without a question.** Lowering the quantity takes free pieces first. As long as the free
   ones cover it, nothing is asked: the counter steps, removing shows the toast with "Rückgängig" (the app has that
   today: every path action returns an undo).
2. **The moment a removal cuts into assigned pieces, a dialog asks before anything changes.** It names the item,
   offers a stepper "Wie viele entfernen?" (1 … wished quantity, preset to what the user triggered: the whole wish
   for "Entfernen", otherwise the step), and shows live what follows: "2 frei · 1 aus Operation „Bodentrupp
   ausrüsten“". Buttons: "Abbrechen" and one action "3 entfernen". A modal dialog closes only by X, Abbrechen or
   Escape (project rule), never by a click beside it.
3. **Several Operationen hold the item:** one row per Operation with its own small stepper, the rows must add up to
   the needed amount. Preset: the most recently assigned Operation gives first (like an undo), the user can shift.
4. **An Operation that loses its last wish:** the dialog says so before the click. If the Operation has Einsätze
   (planned or done), it stays without a wish, and an unticked checkbox offers "Operation auch auflösen". If it has
   no Einsatz at all, it is an empty shell and is dissolved along with the wish; the dialog says "wird mit aufgelöst"
   and the action button takes the danger tone (red is reserved for destructive actions in this app).
5. **Undo:** the toast after the dialog carries "Rückgängig" and restores the wish, the assignments and a dissolved
   Operation in one step.
6. **From the HQ side** (taking a wish out of an Operation, or dissolving the Operation): the wish stays a wish and
   becomes free again, nothing is asked, toast with "Rückgängig".
7. **A wish is fulfilled** (the item arrives in Inventar or Hangar): the Operation's line is ticked as reached and
   stays visible there, it is not removed. No question.
8. Same dialog wherever a wish can be removed or lowered: the wishlist row, the cart counter on a Codex detail page,
   the wish button of the path.

Analytics (project rule, name it in the intro in one sentence): assigning stays `hq:path_group_assign { surface,
created }` from round 2; releasing through this dialog is a new event `hq:path_group_release { surface, pieces,
dissolved }`. Report: the funnel "wish → assigned → fulfilled" on the HQ dashboard in PostHog.

## Facts you may cite (verified in the code; invent no others)
