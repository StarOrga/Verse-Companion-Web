## Settled in round 3 — "Fest steht", never ask again

Six of seven analysis decisions were answered (five as recommended):

- Free pieces go first, without a question (`yes`).
- When a removal cuts into assigned pieces, the dialog lets the user choose the quantity (`menge`).
- Several Operationen: the most recently assigned one gives first, adjustable (`neueste`).
- An Operation that loses its last wish stays when it has Einsätze, otherwise it is dissolved along (`einsaetze`).
- A fulfilled wish stays in the Operation as reached (`yes`).
- **Releasing a wish in the HQ: the user answered `no` to "ohne Nachfrage"** — so releasing in the HQ asks. No note
  was given; this round shows a short confirmation and asks when it should appear.

"sehr gut!"; `entfernen-letzter` → "sehr gut!"; `entfernen-aufgeloest` → "Sehr gut!". Keep those three dialogs as
they are, apart from the split bar (below). `pfad-liste` is settled and is **not** part of this round.

on `pfad-schiff` about the word stayed empty). The user does use "Operation" in their own notes. Keep "Operation"
in all mocks and ask once more, in the smallest possible form (see the analysis view).
